Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

myJungle (“the App”) helps you track plant watering schedules and related care data. This page explains what information is processed when you use the App.

Data we store

Plant profiles, photos, schedules, settings, and optional feedback you submit are stored on your device and/or transmitted only as needed to provide features you use (for example AI analysis or feedback delivery).

Your choices

You can export or reset app data from Settings. You may also disable notifications in Settings or through your device system preferences.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the in-app feedback form or contact the operator listed in the Legal Notice & Impressum.
